EpiGraph-AI

A deep learning pipeline that forecasts dengue outbreaks as a connected, spatio-temporal problem, combining clinical and weather data with local health news. This is a work in progress.

Role
solo or team? confirm
Stack
Python, PyTorch, GAT, LSTM, BioBERT, PCA
Links
GitHub

Problem

Dengue case counts in one district depend on its neighbours, on recent weather, and on signals that show up in local health news before they show up in the numbers. A forecast that uses only one of those misses the rest. The aim is to predict outbreaks early enough to act on. add who acts on the forecast and how much lead time matters.

Users

name the intended user (e.g. district public-health surveillance teams) and whether any were consulted.

Constraints

  • Sudden spikes. Outbreak data has huge outliers that can dominate a squared-error loss.
  • Embedding size. BioBERT embeddings are 768-dimensional, which is too large to fuse efficiently alongside a handful of weather and case features.
  • Data. Three inputs are needed: processed cases with weather variables, news headlines per date and district, and a district connectivity matrix. add data sources, number of districts and time span.

What I built

  • News signal: BioBERT encodes headlines into 768-dim embeddings, compressed to 32 dims with PCA.
  • Spatial signal: an adjacency matrix of district connectivity, modelled with a Graph Attention Network (GAT).
  • Temporal signal: an LSTM over rolling 7-day windows of weather, past cases and news embeddings, predicting one day ahead.
  • Training: one model (EpiGraphModel) handles all three, optimised with Huber loss and early stopping, then evaluated on unseen data.

Metrics

Success was defined before the model was built: MAE, RMSE and outbreak detection accuracy on unseen data.

add the measured MAE, RMSE and detection accuracy, ideally next to a simple baseline such as LSTM-only.

Trade-offs I made

  • Huber loss instead of MSE.It stays robust to sudden case spikes, at the cost of penalising large misses less heavily.
  • PCA from 768 to 32 dimensions.The news signal becomes cheap enough to fuse, at the cost of some of the information in the embedding.
  • 7-day lookback, 1-day horizon.explain why this window and horizon.
