Madhar Sha Customer Intelligence

A Phase 1 preview of customer intelligence for a retail showroom. It turns camera views of the shop floor into a short report the owner can act on: when the shop is actually busy, which sections hold shoppers, and where they wait with nobody serving them.

Stack
Computer vision, static Next.js build
Links
Live demo

Problem

A shop owner can see the floor but not the pattern. Staff are placed by habit rather than by when shoppers actually arrive, some sections hold attention much longer than others, and shoppers can stand in a section with nobody nearby while staff are pulled to the till.

Users

The owner and floor managers of the Madhar Sha showroom.

Constraints

  • The preview is not yet connected to the showroom's own cameras, so every figure shown is illustrative.
  • Until then, the footage and annotations come from public datasets: CARR (Customer Activity Recognition in Retail, CC BY 4.0) and retail_gaze.
  • The readers are shop staff, not analysts.

What I built

A static dashboard with a "yesterday at a glance" summary (visitors, fullest moment, typical stop in a section) and insight cards for the busiest hour, the section that holds shoppers longest, time a section had shoppers but no staff, and billing-counter pressure by hour.

Metrics

No real metrics yet. The preview's figures are illustrative.

Trade-offs I made

  • Plain-language advice over charts.Every number comes with a sentence saying what to do, such as putting the most knowledgeable staff near the section shoppers linger in. The owner doesn't have to interpret a chart.
  • Honest labelling.The preview says clearly that its figures are illustrative, rather than presenting demo data as the shop's own.

What I'd do next

  • Connect the pipeline to the showroom's own cameras so the figures are real.
