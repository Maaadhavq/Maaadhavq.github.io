Mandate Retry SequencerRecovering failed UPI Autopay debits. The agent proposes, compliance rules dispose. ₹44.25Lrecovered of ₹1.26Cr
Problem
Retrying a failed mandate debit is easy. Proving which retries were allowed, which were refused and what the system chose not to do is the hard part.
What I built
A LightGBM recoverability score, five hard compliance rules checked before and after every decision, and an LLM agent confined to the ambiguous score band. Every rupee lands in an append-only ledger.
Result
₹44.25L of ₹1.26Cr at risk recovered (35%) across 500 synthetic records. 187 records were stopped by a rule, 11 agent proposals were vetoed, and none of 266 debits ran in an NPCI peak window. 214 tests.
Product decision
No score and no agent proposal can override a rule. I also list every unrecovered record in full rather than showing only the headline number.

PAD Detection + Risk CopilotPredicting peripheral artery disease from hospital data, then explaining the score with sources a reader can check. 0.88AUC, best model
Problem
A risk score on its own is of little use to a reader who can't check why it came out that way.
What I built
A leakage-controlled pipeline over MIMIC-IV (13 features, 6 classifiers) and a local RAG copilot that explains each score with SHAP attributions and cited passages.
Result
Best AUC 0.88. The copilot retrieves the right passage for 24 of 24 golden questions, and no unresolvable citation survives. 54 tests.
Product decision
Citations are verified in code, not requested in a prompt. If nothing relevant is retrieved, the copilot says so instead of answering.

Python · scikit-learn · XGBoost · LightGBM · SHAP · Ollama · Streamlit

EpiGraph-AIForecasting dengue outbreaks across connected districts from cases, weather and local news. WIPmetrics to come
