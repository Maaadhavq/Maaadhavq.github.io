Featured projects

Mandate Retry Sequencer

Fintech · AI agent

Recovering failed UPI Autopay debits, where the agent proposes and compliance rules dispose.

Problem
Retrying a failed mandate debit is easy. Proving which retries were allowed, which were refused and what the system chose not to do is the hard part.
What I built
A LightGBM recoverability score, five hard compliance rules checked before and after every decision, and an LLM agent confined to the ambiguous score band. Every rupee lands in an append-only ledger.
Result
₹44.25L of ₹1.26Cr at risk recovered (35%) across 500 synthetic records, with zero of 266 debits run inside an NPCI peak window.
Product decision
No score and no agent proposal can override a rule. I also list every unrecovered record in full rather than showing only the headline number.

PAD Detection + Risk Copilot

Health · RAG

Predicting peripheral artery disease from routine hospital data, then explaining the score with sources a reader can check.

Problem
A risk score on its own is of little use to a reader who cannot check why it came out that way.
What I built
A leakage-controlled pipeline over MIMIC-IV (13 features, 6 classifiers) and a local RAG copilot that explains each score with SHAP and cited passages.
Result
Best AUC 0.88. The copilot retrieves the right passage for 24 of 24 golden questions, and no unresolvable citation survives.
Product decision
Citations are verified in code, not requested in a prompt. If nothing relevant is retrieved, the copilot says so instead of answering.

EpiGraph-AI

Public health · GNN

Forecasting dengue outbreaks across connected districts from cases, weather and local news.

Problem
Outbreaks spread between connected districts and show up in local news before case counts, and single-signal forecasts miss both.
What I built
A hybrid GAT + LSTM model that fuses district connectivity, 7-day case and weather history, and BioBERT news embeddings.
Result
Work in progress. Success is defined up front as MAE, RMSE and outbreak detection accuracy. add measured values
Product decision
