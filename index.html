Selected Projects

EpiGraph-AI — Epidemic Outbreak Prediction

GitHub ↗

A hybrid deep-learning architecture combining Graph Attention Networks, LSTMs, and BioBERT to forecast dengue outbreaks from spatio-temporal clinical, weather, and news data. News headlines are embedded via BioBERT (768→32 dims) and fused into a multi-modal pipeline; inter-district spread is modelled with graph adjacency matrices over 7-day rolling windows.
